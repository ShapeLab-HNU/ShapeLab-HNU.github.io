ShapeLab is a research group at Hunan University led by Prof. Ruihui Li.

Our research focuses on:

- 3D Vision
- Computer Graphics
- Embodied Intelligence
- Large Models
- 3D Scene Understanding
- 3D Reconstruction

## News

- **2026-01** ShapeLab website launched.

See more on the [News](/news/) page.

## Research Areas

### 3D Vision
Understanding 3D scenes and geometry from images and point clouds.

### Computer Graphics
3D generation, reconstruction, and rendering.

### Embodied Intelligence
Perception and reasoning for intelligent agents.

### Large Models for 3D
Vision-language models and 3D foundation models.
